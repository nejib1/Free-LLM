## About

BazaarLink is a Taiwanese OpenAI-compatible AI gateway (operated by 集聯科技有限公司) with 120+ models and a daily free tier that needs no credit card: 10 requests/minute and 60 weighted request units per day (20 RPM / 120 units once you hold any credit), 2 concurrent free requests per account, reset at 00:00 UTC. Free models rotate; at the time of writing they include Qwen3.7 Flash, Reflection Beam, Nemotron 3.5 Lightning, Glyph Cluster and DeepSeek V4 Flash. Small, young provider: verify before relying on it.
