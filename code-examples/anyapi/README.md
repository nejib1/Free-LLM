## About

AnyAPI is an OpenAI-compatible gateway to 400+ models. Its Free plan ($0/month, no credit card) gives 100K "ANY tokens" per day on every model marked free in its catalog (for example Qwen3.8 27B, Ling 3.0 Flash and Gemma 3n at the time of writing). The exact free model ids are only shown in the logged-in catalog. Paid models, including closed ones, are pay-as-you-go from $20.
